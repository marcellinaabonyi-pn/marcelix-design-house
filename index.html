Hi, I'm Marcellina

I design brands people remember

I'm Marcellina Abonyi, founder and designer at Marcelix Design House in New York. I help small businesses, from coffee shops and restaurants to skincare, fitness and property brands, look as good as what they actually sell.

Every brand starts with one simple idea. A sun setting over the sea. Bubbles rising in a can. Three bowls stacked on a studio shelf. From there I build everything else around it: the logo, colours, type, packaging, stationery and social posts, so your business feels like one clear brand wherever people see it.

I also build photo-realistic 3D mockups in Blender, so you can see your brand on real products before you spend a dollar on printing.

FAQ

Good
questions

The things people usually ask before we start.

I have no brand yet. Where do we start? +

With a short chat about your business, who you sell to and what makes you different. From there I recommend a package. Most new businesses start with Brand Identity Essentials.

How do concepts and revisions work? +

I present a set of concepts, you pick the one that feels right, and we refine it together. The number of concepts and revision rounds depends on your package, and I confirm it before we begin.

What files do I get? +

Print-ready PDF, SVG and PNG files for every logo version, your colour codes and fonts, plus the 3D mockup images to use on your website and social media.

What are the 3D mockups? +

Photo-realistic renders of your brand on real products, like cans, bags, signs and business cards. They let you see the brand in the real world before you pay for printing, and they make great launch images.

Do you work with clients outside the US? +

Yes. I'm based in New York and work with businesses anywhere. Everything is shared online, and calls are booked at a time that works for both of us.
